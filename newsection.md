## **Final design and content recommendation**

For the exact carousel design you provided, keep the visible card content minimal: **service name + one short emotional benefit + arrow icon**. Avoid placing long descriptions over the photos.

Use a consistent structure across all dedicated service pages: hero, introduction, benefits, training experience, intended audience, and final CTA. This creates a more professional website and makes it easier for visitors to understand and compare your gym's offerings.

Before publishing, verify that the gym actually offers the classes, trainers, equipment, dedicated zones, and progress reviews described. The copy is designed as a professional starting point, not a factual audit of the facility.
