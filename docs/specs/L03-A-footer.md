# L03-A: Footer

Status: **verified for the documented scope**. Date: 2026-11-16. Item 4 of the [13-item pre-Home checklist](../pre-home-checklist.md).

## Purpose and boundary

Provide a server-rendered footer on every exported page and the custom 404, using supplied business details and existing destinations. This iteration builds and mounts only the footer. The preview notice, shared contact-detail abstraction, page sections, forms, dependency updates and launch approval remain separate.

The documented `figma/footer.png` reference is absent from the working tree. Use the agreed charcoal surface and verified foundation roles; visual approval remains open. Preserve originals in `info/`, all current assets, business records and routes.

## Content and component contract

- `Footer()` is a Server Component with no public props or local state. Mount it after the existing `main#main`, outside main, with one contentinfo landmark. Preserve the banner, first-tab skip link, page H1, route shells and preview indexing protections.
- Reuse BrandLink and NavigationLink. Reuse all four groups/15 destinations from the validated `src/content/navigation.ts`, in the same order, inside a separately named “Footer navigation” landmark with semantic headings/lists. Footer navigation is always expanded and available without JavaScript; current-page matching remains owned by the header.
- A validated `src/content/footer.ts` record derives the exact address, displayed phone, normalized telephone, Facebook and Instagram URLs from `site.ts`. Do not duplicate business values in JSX. Keep the original `info/Crossfit Gym_info.md` unchanged. Directions reuse the existing address-based Maps search URL, not a verified map pin.
- Show the real address in a nonitalic semantic address element, a native `tel:+8801323275695` link labeled `01323-275695`, “Get directions”, “Facebook” and “Instagram”. HTTP(S) links use the same tab by default; no external service request is made before activation. No email or opening times were supplied. Show “Opening hours have not been supplied. Call to confirm.” as an explicit availability note. Omit unsupported marketing/copyright claims and subscription UI.
- Add a separate “Legal” navigation containing Privacy `/privacy` and Terms `/terms`, validated against registered destinations. Their routes exist but approved legal text is absent. Show “Legal text is awaiting approval.” while either document is unapproved; do not imply release approval. This is distinct from the later site-wide preview notice.
- New structured footer content is validated with Zod: nonempty copy, normalized telephone syntax, HTTPS channel destinations, nonempty unique social labels/URLs and registered legal links. Existing content values and approval flags remain unchanged. No new real/preview catalog records are added.
- Use a full-width explicit charcoal surface with a constrained 72rem inner container, existing responsive gutters, 48px vertical padding, 40px main section gap and 24px navigation-group gap. Brand/contact and navigation may sit side by side on large screens; intrinsic navigation columns wrap according to available space/text size. Legal content follows a semantic boundary. Avoid fixed heights, truncation or hidden overflow.
- Group/contact headings use Inter 600 control-emphasis presentation with semantic H2 elements; they are navigation labels rather than display headings. Links have at least 44px targets and preserve the existing yellow link, focus, wrapping and 150ms control-feedback contracts. Address/notes remain readable, including on font/image failure, reduced motion and forced colors.
- Keyboard order follows visual/DOM order: brand, phone/directions, grouped navigation, social and legal links. Contact/social/legal links have meaningful visible names; no icon-only controls are needed. Header modal isolation must also hide the footer while open and release it after dismissal.
